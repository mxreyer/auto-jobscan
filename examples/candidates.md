# Candidates — 2026-08-24

> **Illustrative example.** An eight-role excerpt of what `jobscan.py` writes to
> `candidates.md` at the repo root (gitignored — real output stays local). The
> run behind it returned **124 roles from 2,066 open across 14 sources**.
>
> The **companies, titles, locations and links are real**, fetched on
> 2026-08-24 by `examples/config.json`. The **description text is not**: each
> posting's body is redacted and replaced with filler of the same length, so
> this repository does not republish employers' copy. Follow a link to read the
> real thing — while it lasts; postings expire.
>
> `examples/shortlist.md` scores these eight against `examples/profile.md`, and
> quotes a sentence or two from the originals where a quotation is what makes
> the reasoning checkable.
>
> The eight are chosen to exercise the rubric rather than to sample the run:
> the top of the ranking, one genuinely hybrid role (row 3 — a research
> engineer at a NASA contractor, which triggers the conditional rule in
> `SCORING.md`), and three contrasting Skips.
